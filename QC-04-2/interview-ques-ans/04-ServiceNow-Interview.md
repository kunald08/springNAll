# ServiceNow — Interview Questions & Answers

---

## 1. What is ServiceNow?

**Answer:**
ServiceNow is a cloud-based platform used to manage IT services, workflows, and enterprise processes. It is commonly used for incident management, request handling, change management, and service desk operations.

---

## 2. Why is ServiceNow popular in IT organizations?

**Answer:**
ServiceNow is popular because it centralizes service management processes in one platform. It improves visibility, standardizes workflows, supports automation, and helps teams manage tickets and service operations more efficiently.

---

## 3. What is ServiceNow architecture in simple terms?

**Answer:**
In simple terms, ServiceNow is a cloud-based platform where users access the application through a browser, business logic runs in the platform, and data is stored in platform tables. Organizations often use separate instances for development, testing, and production.

---

## 4. What is the difference between an application and a module in ServiceNow?

**Answer:**
An application is a major functional area such as Incident or Change Management. A module is a menu item inside that application, such as `Create New` or `Assigned to Me`. So application is the bigger area, and modules are specific entry points within it.

---

## 5. What is a record in ServiceNow?

**Answer:**
A record is a single stored item in a ServiceNow table. For example, one incident ticket, one change request, or one user profile is a record.

---

## 6. What is the role of an administrator in ServiceNow?

**Answer:**
A ServiceNow administrator manages users, roles, permissions, forms, lists, workflows, and platform configuration. The administrator ensures that the system is secure, properly customized, and aligned with business requirements.

---

## 7. What is ITSM in ServiceNow?

**Answer:**
ITSM stands for IT Service Management. In ServiceNow, ITSM includes processes such as incident management, problem management, change management, and request management. These processes help organizations deliver and support IT services in a structured way.

---

## 8. What is the difference between incident, problem, and change?

**Answer:**
- **Incident** is about restoring service quickly when something breaks
- **Problem** is about identifying and removing the root cause of recurring incidents
- **Change** is about managing modifications to systems in a controlled and low-risk way

This distinction is important in ITSM because each has a different objective.

---

## 9. What are roles in ServiceNow?

**Answer:**
Roles define what a user is allowed to do in the platform. For example, some users may only view incidents, while administrators can configure the platform. Roles help maintain security and controlled access.

---

## 10. Can you give a real-world example of ServiceNow usage?

**Answer:**
Yes. If an employee cannot access email, a service desk agent creates an incident in ServiceNow. The support team investigates and resolves it. If the same issue keeps happening, a problem record may be created, and if an infrastructure update is needed, a change request may be raised. This shows how ServiceNow supports connected ITSM workflows.
