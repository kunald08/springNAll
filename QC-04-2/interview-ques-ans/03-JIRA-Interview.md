# JIRA — Interview Questions & Answers

---

## 1. What is JIRA?

**Answer:**
JIRA is a project management and issue tracking tool widely used by software teams. It helps teams plan work, track bugs, manage stories and tasks, and monitor progress through boards, workflows, and reports.

---

## 2. What is an issue in JIRA?

**Answer:**
An issue is the basic work item in JIRA. It can represent a bug, task, story, epic, or another work type depending on project configuration. Each issue stores details such as summary, description, assignee, priority, and status.

---

## 3. What are the common issue types in JIRA?

**Answer:**
Common issue types are:

- bug
- task
- story
- epic

In agile projects, stories usually represent user requirements, bugs represent defects, and epics represent larger features made up of multiple smaller issues.

---

## 4. What is a workflow in JIRA?

**Answer:**
A workflow defines the lifecycle of an issue, meaning the statuses it goes through from creation to completion. For example, an issue may move from `To Do` to `In Progress`, then `Testing`, and finally `Done`.

---

## 5. Why are workflows important?

**Answer:**
Workflows are important because they bring structure and consistency. They help teams understand where a task currently stands, who should act next, and what approvals or validations are required before moving it forward.

---

## 6. What is the difference between Scrum and Kanban boards in JIRA?

**Answer:**
A Scrum board is used for sprint-based work, where tasks are planned and completed within a fixed time period. A Kanban board is used for continuous flow, where tasks move through stages without fixed sprints. Scrum focuses on sprint planning, while Kanban focuses more on flow efficiency and limiting work in progress.

---

## 7. What is JIRA administration?

**Answer:**
JIRA administration involves managing the configuration of JIRA. This may include creating projects, managing users and roles, setting permissions, configuring issue types, workflows, screens, fields, and notifications.

---

## 8. What are dashboards in JIRA?

**Answer:**
Dashboards are visual pages that display project information using gadgets or widgets. They help teams and managers quickly see things like sprint progress, issue distribution, unresolved bugs, and team performance.

---

## 9. What are some common reports available in JIRA?

**Answer:**
Some common reports are:

- burndown chart
- sprint report
- velocity chart
- pie chart by issue type
- created vs resolved report

These reports help teams track progress and identify bottlenecks.

---

## 10. How would you explain JIRA in one practical sentence during an interview?

**Answer:**
I would say JIRA is a tool that helps software teams organize, track, and manage work in a transparent way from task creation to completion.
