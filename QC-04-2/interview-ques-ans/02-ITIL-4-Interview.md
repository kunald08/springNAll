# ITIL 4 — Interview Questions & Answers

---

## 1. What is ITIL 4?

**Answer:**
ITIL 4 is a framework of best practices for IT service management. It helps organizations design, deliver, support, and improve IT services in a way that creates value for the business and users.

---

## 2. What is service management?

**Answer:**
Service management is the set of organizational activities used to plan, deliver, operate, and improve services for customers. The main idea is not just to run technology, but to provide outcomes that are useful for the business.

---

## 3. What are the four dimensions of service management in ITIL 4?

**Answer:**
The four dimensions are:

- organizations and people
- information and technology
- partners and suppliers
- value streams and processes

These dimensions ensure that service management is viewed in a complete and balanced way.

---

## 4. What is the Service Value System in ITIL 4?

**Answer:**
The Service Value System, or SVS, shows how all components of an organization work together to create value. It includes guiding principles, governance, the service value chain, practices, and continual improvement.

---

## 5. What is the Service Value Chain?

**Answer:**
The Service Value Chain is the operating model of ITIL 4. It includes six key activities:

- plan
- improve
- engage
- design and transition
- obtain or build
- deliver and support

These activities work together to convert demand into value.

---

## 6. What is the difference between a process and a practice in ITIL 4?

**Answer:**
A process is a defined sequence of steps to achieve a result. A practice is broader and includes people, tools, skills, and processes. In ITIL 4, the term practice is preferred because it reflects how work actually happens in real organizations.

---

## 7. What are ITIL 4 guiding principles?

**Answer:**
The guiding principles are general recommendations that support decision-making. Examples include:

- focus on value
- start where you are
- progress iteratively with feedback
- collaborate and promote visibility
- keep it simple and practical
- optimize and automate

These principles are useful across almost all IT service situations.

---

## 8. What is continual improvement in ITIL 4?

**Answer:**
Continual improvement means regularly identifying and implementing ways to improve services, processes, and performance. The goal is to make services more efficient, reliable, and valuable over time instead of treating improvement as a one-time activity.

---

## 9. Why is ITIL important for organizations?

**Answer:**
ITIL is important because it improves service quality, creates consistency, reduces operational issues, and aligns IT services with business goals. It also helps teams work with a structured approach instead of handling incidents and changes in an unorganized way.

---

## 10. Can you give a simple example of ITIL in practice?

**Answer:**
Yes. Suppose users report that an application is slow. The service desk logs incidents, the support team investigates the issue, problem management identifies the root cause, change management approves the fix, and continual improvement checks whether the solution improved service quality. That is a practical ITIL-based flow.
